About Two Lane Journal

Two Lane Journal is a small, independent reader about road trips, scenic drives and traveling at a slower pace. We started it after one unplanned detour turned a routine drive home into the best part of a weekend.

Everything here comes from our own trips and from letters readers send in. We do not run sponsored posts and we do not sell anything. If a guide mentions a diner, a bookstore or an overlook, it is because we went there and liked it.

What you will find here

  • Routes: drives we have done, with the honest time cost and the stops worth making.
  • Guides: small, practical pieces on planning, packing and finding good food on the road.
  • Gear: the few things we actually keep in the car.
  • Reader notes: letters from people who tried a route and wrote back.

Thanks for reading. Take the slow road.
